Media Estimator

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Overview

Media Estimator is designed with your privacy in mind. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

Media Estimator does not collect any data from its users. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • No usage data is collected (analytics, diagnostics, crash reports, etc.)
  • No tracking of any kind is performed
  • No cookies or similar technologies are used
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are included

Data Storage

All project data you create within Media Estimator is stored locally on your device. Your data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

Subscriptions

Media Estimator offers optional in-app subscriptions processed entirely through Apple's App Store. Subscription transactions are handled by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Media Estimator does not have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

Media Estimator does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

Media Estimator does not collect any data from any user, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Since no data is collected, meaningful changes to this policy are unlikely.
